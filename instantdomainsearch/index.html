      How It Works & Privacy

      Do you log or sell domain searches?

      No. The domain names you type are never logged, stored, or shared with registrars or brokers. Only coarse country-level ending counts (such as .ca or .com) are tallied, with the domain name itself discarded.

      How is availability verified?

      Delegated names resolve immediately via DNS (NS/SOA) at the edge. Any name without DNS records is verified live against the authoritative registry RDAP server so reserved, redemption, and serverHold domains aren’t falsely shown as available.

      Which domain endings are supported?

      Over 550 public endings including .com, .ai, .io, .dev, .app, .ca, .co.uk, .de, .com.au, and .si. Press . in the search bar or click the ending pill to filter all supported endings.

      Where do Register links go?

      Choose your preferred registrar from the dropdown on any register button — Namecheap, Cloudflare Registrar, Porkbun, or GoDaddy. Your choice is saved locally in your browser, or you can copy any available domain with one click.
